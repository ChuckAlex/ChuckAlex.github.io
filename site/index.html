Research Interests

Robot Learning

How can a robot learn a skill from demonstrations and refine it through interaction? I’m interested in how vision–language–action (VLA) models and reinforcement learning can work together.

Physical World Models

I want to predict how a scene would change if a robot took a different action. That question draws me to world models and causal prediction.

Real-World Interaction

I’m interested in agentic systems that can notice when a grasp or a route has gone off plan, use fresh observations and decide what to do next.

Oct. 2024–May 2025

Research Intern

Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences

Deepfake Detection & Data Engineering

I built a pipeline to prepare face crops from three deepfake video datasets and helped develop CLIP baselines to compare how well different visual encoders detected forgeries on Celeb-DF. In a separate collaboration with Ant Group, I deployed two image generators on an offline GPU server to produce samples for the IJCAI 2025 Deepfake Workshop challenge.

Read the ID³ paperIET Biometrics · 2025 · Fourth author
Explore the detection experiments and challenge dataset
Preserving visual evidence in face crops
I extracted frames from Celeb-DF, DFDC and DeeperForensics and aligned faces using MTCNN landmarks, refining difficult cases with dlib’s 68-point landmarks. I kept a margin around each face to preserve potential manipulation artifacts near its edges and linked each crop to its source video.
Testing CLIP detection baselines
I compared visual backbones, including ViT-B/16 and ViT-L/14, using AUC and accuracy on the Celeb-DF test set. I also tested inputs assembled from 3, 5 or 7 consecutive frames to examine how the choice of frames affected detection.
Generating the challenge dataset with Ant Group
I deployed RDDM and StyleGAN-XL without internet access on the server. After resolving dependency conflicts and fixing inference output paths, I wrote multi-GPU batch inference scripts and organized the generated images under a common naming scheme for delivery.

Mar.–Aug. 2024

Research Intern

Laboratory of Intelligent Technology and Systems, Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University

Robot Arm Calibration, Pose Estimation & Control

I calibrated an external RealSense camera against an RM-65 robot arm and built a Raspberry Pi interface to command the arm and read its feedback. I also controlled a gripper on a real-time embedded platform and reproduced Gen6D for 6-DoF object pose estimation.

Watch the robot grasp an object
Explore calibration, motion control and pose estimation
Relating camera observations to robot coordinates
I mounted an ArUco marker on the wrist and moved the arm through a set of poses. For each pose, I paired the fixed RealSense D435i camera’s observation with the end-effector pose reported by the controller. I used these pairs to estimate the transform between the camera and the robot base in ROS Noetic.
Commanding the arm and gripper
On the Raspberry Pi, I used JSON over TCP to send joint-space moves (MoveJ) and straight-line end-effector moves (MoveL), then parsed the controller’s replies. On a Loongson 2K1000 running ReWorks, I wrote serial and GPIO drivers in C and sent gripper commands over the serial interface.
Reproducing Gen6D
I set up the PyTorch data and inference pipeline for Gen6D, which estimates an object’s 3D position and orientation without a 3D model of that object. I ran inference on multiple objects and also used Open3D and PCL to convert RGB-D observations into point clouds.

Mar.–Sept. 2023

Flight-Control Software Lead

Quadrotor Flight Control & Autonomous Task Scheduling

I developed the quadrotor’s main flight-control program in C on a TM4C123GH6PM. The controller stabilized the aircraft while a task state machine used range measurements and visual cues to manage takeoff, waypoint flight, hovering and landing. The recording shows the quadrotor flying and hovering indoors in autonomous mode.

Watch autonomous indoor flight and hovering
Explore flight control and task scheduling
Keeping the aircraft stable
I used a complementary filter to estimate attitude from inertial sensor readings. The attitude controller used PID feedback to correct deviations from the commanded orientation, while range measurements provided feedback for altitude control.
Using vision to advance a task
OpenMV sent recognition results to the flight controller over a serial link. I wrote the state-transition logic so that the same sensor input could be interpreted in the context of the current flight phase, determining when to hold a state and when to start the next task.
Operating and debugging the aircraft
I implemented switching between manual and autonomous modes and a serial link to the ground station. During testing, I could send commands and read back the active state to check when the aircraft switched modes or advanced to the next phase.

Research & Engineering Capabilities

Robot Learning & Visual Perception

I developed a VLA post-training method for humanoid manipulation and evaluated how well deepfake detectors transfer across video datasets at the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Algorithm Design & Evaluation

I designed IRC-RS to teach a navigation policy to account for moving obstacles, then compared its behavior with a control policy in reproducible encounters.

Robot Learning Data Pipelines

I built a pipeline that turns first-person video, UMI recordings and robot teleoperation logs into 20 Hz LeRobot datasets, with masks for missing observations and motion labels.

Sim2Real & Robot Deployment

I deployed a navigation policy trained in simulation on a differential-drive robot and integrated multi-view cameras, robot state and ROS 2 control for humanoid manipulation.
